# Choose a Workflow

Choose the smallest Skill that creates the evidence or artifact you need. Combine Skills only when one output becomes the next Skill's bounded input.

| Goal | Skill | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current market, competitor, technology, policy, company, and product research | [SearchCans Deep Research](SearchCans-Deep-Research) | A bounded, evidence-led brief with traceable URLs, conflicts, and uncertainty |
| Keyword research, search-intent analysis, competitor-page review, and content opportunity planning | [SearchCans SERP Content Gap](SearchCans-SERP-Content-Gap) | A localized Google or Bing SERP opportunity brief grounded in observed result features |
| Web-to-Markdown extraction, RAG input checks, dynamic pages, PDFs, Office files, and page-level SEO/GEO review | [SearchCans Reader SEO Audit](SearchCans-Reader-SEO-Audit) | An extraction report with observable canonical, H1, meta-description, JSON-LD, file, and screenshot signals |
| Competitor, category, launch, PR, and news monitoring with optional snapshot comparison | [SearchCans Market Watch](SearchCans-Market-Watch) | A Google, Google News, Bing, and Reader evidence snapshot with URL-level baseline differences |
| E-commerce category research, merchant discovery, product-page planning, and localized merchandising briefs | [SearchCans Product SERP Brief](SearchCans-Product-SERP-Brief) | A Google Shopping, web, images, and optional merchant-page evidence brief |
| Editorial format discovery, visual/video search research, and SEO/GEO content format planning | [SearchCans Content Format Brief](SearchCans-Content-Format-Brief) | A localized inventory of Google web, images, videos, and short-video result formats |
| Grounding packs, source curation, knowledge-base intake, and pre-ingestion evidence checks | [SearchCans RAG Source Curator](SearchCans-RAG-Source-Curator) | A small, domain-diverse Reader/File source manifest with evidence-readiness gating |

## Selection rules

- Use **Deep Research** when an answer needs multiple read sources, a 3–5 question plan, and a formal evidence gate.
- Use **SERP Content Gap** for one localized keyword/SERP decision; use **Content Format Brief** when images and video formats are part of the decision.
- Use **Reader SEO Audit** for one known URL or file; use **RAG Source Curator** to find and select a small source set first.
- Use **Market Watch** for repeatable market/news snapshots; use **Product SERP Brief** for market-specific product and merchant observations.

Do not treat SERP snippets or result appearance as proof. Read a page before using it for a consequential claim.
