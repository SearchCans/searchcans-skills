# SearchCans Market Watch

> A Google, Google News, Bing, and Reader evidence snapshot with URL-level baseline differences

## Best for

Competitor, category, launch, PR, and news monitoring with optional snapshot comparison.

## SearchCans APIs used

- Google Search
- Google News
- Bing Search
- Reader
- Account

## Account-aware behavior

Checks one pre-flight, caps selected Reader news sources, and respects reported lane count.

## Use it

```text
Use $searchcans-market-watch to create a US market snapshot for this category.
```

Read the executable [SKILL.md](https://github.com/SearchCans/searchcans-skills/tree/main/skills/searchcans-market-watch) for supported flags, evidence boundaries, and report guidance.

## Interpretation boundary

Build a current, geo-targeted market-watch snapshot from Google Search, Google News, Bing Search, and selected Reader extracts. Use for competitor and category monitoring, PR/news tracking, launch intelligence, and URL-level change checks between two bounded runs.
