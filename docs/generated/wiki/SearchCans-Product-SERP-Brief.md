# SearchCans Product SERP Brief

> A Google Shopping, web, images, and optional merchant-page evidence brief

## Best for

E-commerce category research, merchant discovery, product-page planning, and localized merchandising briefs.

## SearchCans APIs used

- Google Shopping
- Google Search
- Google Images
- Reader
- Account

## Account-aware behavior

Checks the three SERP calls plus explicit Reader URLs and caps only source reads when needed.

## Use it

```text
Use $searchcans-product-serp-brief to research this product category in the US.
```

Read the executable [SKILL.md](https://github.com/SearchCans/searchcans-skills/tree/main/skills/searchcans-product-serp-brief) for supported flags, evidence boundaries, and report guidance.

## Interpretation boundary

Create a localized product-search evidence brief from Google Shopping, Google web results, Google Images, and optional Reader extracts of explicit merchant URLs. Use for e-commerce category research, competitor assortment discovery, product-page planning, and market-specific merchandising briefs.
