# Content Brief Standard

## Interpret the SERP

- Group organic results by page type and user intent: explanatory, comparison, commercial, documentation, news, or navigational.
- List only observations that appear in the evidence pack. Link each competing page by URL.
- Use PAA and related searches as question candidates, not proof of demand volume.
- Keep news separate from evergreen intent.

## Recommend content

- Match the dominant intent before proposing differentiation.
- Identify missing buyer questions, unsupported claims, weak source coverage, or absent comparison criteria.
- Provide an outline that answers the observed questions without copying competitor wording.
- Flag items that need Reader extraction before they become factual assertions.
